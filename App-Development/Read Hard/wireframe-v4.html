4. Misplaced Books Mechanic (harder levels)

How it works:
Level 1–3: Only shelve books from cart
Level 4+: Some shelves have misplaced books on them
A misplaced book pulses with an orange ! above it

To fix: walk to it → SPACE → pick it up
It goes to your cart (same inventory)
Carry it to the correct section → SPACE → shelve it

Why it's hard:
• You now have MORE books on your cart
• More trips across the maze
• Librarian has more chances to catch you
• Timer is already shorter on hard levels

Score bonus for fixing misplaced books!

Book glow states:

Normal book — correctly placed, nothing to do
Misplaced book — orange glow + ! above it
Your cart book just placed — green flash
Wrong shelf — red flash then resets
