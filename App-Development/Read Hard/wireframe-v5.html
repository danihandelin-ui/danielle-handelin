SPACE — Pick Up / Put Down

1
Walk Dewey near a shelf.
Cart glows green = you have a book for here.
2
Press SPACE → Dewey reaches into cart,
picks up the book. A small card flashes
the title + call number for 1 second.
3
Walk to the exact shelf section.
Press SPACE again → Dewey places it.
✓
Right shelf → book snaps in, green flash, score!
Book disappears from your hand + cart.
✗
Wrong shelf → red flash, book returns to cart.
−2 seconds taken from your timer.
Misplaced book on shelf:
Stand near it → SPACE to pick it up
Adds to your cart. Deliver to correct section.
+bonus points for fixing misplaced books!
