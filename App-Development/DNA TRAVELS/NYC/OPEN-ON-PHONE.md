## Add to your home screen (one time)
1. Open the link above in **Safari** (iPhone) or **Chrome** (Android).
2. Tap the **Share** button → **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Tap **Add**. You now have a DNA TRAVELS app icon.

## Good to know
- Your schedule **auto-saves** to this phone and is there when you come back.
- The **Save trip** button downloads a backup file if you ever want one.
- Use **only this live link** — the version on the computer is a separate saved copy.
- **Nearby** centers on the stops you've planned for the day (not your live GPS).
