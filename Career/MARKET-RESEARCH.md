## Market Trends (2026)

### What's Hot

✅ **Experiential events** are growing → high-end venues need skilled coordinators
✅ **Operations automation** is hot → venues want people who understand systems
✅ **Sustainability** is important → operations roles focused on reducing waste
✅ **Tech-savvy operators** are valued → people who know Jira, automation, data

### Compensation Drivers

**Higher pay for:**
- Multi-site experience (you managed 4 locations? That's valuable)
- Systems/process design (not just executing, but designing)
- Staff training/development background
- Tech skills (Jira, HubSpot, data tools, automation)
- Scrum/Agile certification
- Consulting experience (freelance/project work)

**Lower pay for:**
- Event coordination only (if that's your only skill)
- Single-site experience
- No systems/design work
- Manual/reactive operations

## How to Research Venues

### Step 1: Find Local Venues
- Google: "[Your city] event venues"
- Google: "[Your city] country clubs"
- Google: "[Your city] luxury hotels"
- LinkedIn: Search company names + look at employee profiles

### Step 2: Check Job Boards
- LinkedIn: Search "Event Coordinator [Your City]"
- Indeed.com: Filter by location and role
- Glassdoor: See salaries + reviews

### Step 3: Visit Their Websites
- Look for "Careers" or "Jobs" pages
- Check if they list openings
- Note: Contact info for hiring managers (often in LinkedIn)

### Step 4: Network
- Connect on LinkedIn with event managers at target venues
- Attend local chamber of commerce meetings
- Reach out for informational interviews
