## Skill Gap 1: Lean Six Sigma Green Belt

### Recommended Path (PRIMARY)

**Lean Six Sigma Institute Online Green Belt**
- **Cost:** $1,200-1,500
- **Duration:** 4-6 weeks, self-paced (5-8 hours/week)
- **URL:** https://www.leansixsigmainstitute.org/
- **What's included:** 
  - Online courses (DMAIC methodology, process mapping, statistics)
  - Exam prep materials
  - Green Belt certification upon completion
- **Best for:** Fast, flexible, recognized credential
- **Why this one:** Affordable, flexible timing (finish before month 9 ops role), industry-recognized, includes everything you need
- **Your timeline:** Enroll Month 6 → Complete Month 9

### Alternative Path (Premium Support)

**UC Irvine Lean Six Sigma Green Belt (Online)**
- **Cost:** $2,000-2,500
- **Duration:** 6-8 weeks, self-paced with structured curriculum
- **URL:** https://www.ucielearning.org/
- **What's included:**
  - Video lectures and case studies
  - Weekly assignments with feedback
  - Dedicated instructor support
  - Capstone project (real-world application)
  - Green Belt certification
- **Best for:** If you want more structure and feedback
- **Your timeline:** Enroll Month 6 → Complete Month 10

### Quick Start Alternative

**Coursera Lean Six Sigma Specialization**
- **Cost:** $800-1,200 (one-time or monthly audit)
- **Duration:** 3-4 months, self-paced
- **URL:** https://www.coursera.org/specializations/lean-six-sigma (search for "Lean Six Sigma")
- **What's included:**
  - 4-course specialization
  - Video lectures
  - Quizzes and projects
  - Certificate upon completion
- **Pros:** Can audit individual courses first ($0) to test before committing
- **Cons:** Certificate not as formally recognized as institutional Green Belt

### Books to Supplement

**"The Lean Six Sigma Pocket Toolbook" by George et al.**
- **Cost:** $20 (Amazon)
- **Best for:** Reference guide while taking course; visual learning
- **What it covers:** Tools, metrics, checklists

**"The Goal" by Eliyahu M. Goldratt**
- **Cost:** $15 (Amazon)
- **Best for:** Understanding continuous improvement mindset
- **What it covers:** Systems thinking, bottlenecks, improvement philosophy

## Skill Gap 2: Financial Literacy

### Free Resources (Start Here)

**Khan Academy - Finance and Capital Markets**
- **Cost:** FREE
- **URL:** https://www.khanacademy.org/finance-and-capital-markets
- **What's covered:** 
  - Basic accounting
  - Income statements (P&L)
  - Cash flow
  - Business metrics
- **Best for:** Foundational understanding
- **Time needed:** 10-15 hours to cover key sections

**YouTube Channels (FREE)**
- "Restaurant Finance 101" (search YouTube)
- "Hospitality Finance Basics" (search YouTube)
- "P&L for Managers" (search YouTube)
- **Best for:** Quick 5-10 minute explanations

**Ask Your Partner (FREE)**
- **Best resource for you:** Your partner works in tech/Agile (likely understands P&L, metrics, ROI)
- **What to ask:** 
  - "Can you explain a P&L statement to me?"
  - "How do tech companies think about labor costs and ROI?"
  - "What metrics matter most to you in your work?"
- **Time needed:** 2-3 sessions, 30 minutes each

### Structured Courses ($200-500)

**Udemy: "Restaurant Accounting & Financial Management"**
- **Cost:** $13-50 (often on sale)
- **Duration:** 3-4 weeks, 5 hours/week
- **URL:** Search "Restaurant Accounting" on Udemy
- **What's covered:**
  - Reading P&L
  - Food cost %
  - Labor cost %
  - Budgeting and financial controls
  - ROI calculation
- **Best for:** Hospitality-specific finance learning

**Coursera: "Finance for Non-Financial Professionals"**
- **Cost:** $39-79/month to audit; $500+ for certificate
- **Duration:** 4-6 weeks
- **URL:** https://www.coursera.org/ (search "Finance for Non-Financial")
- **What's covered:**
  - Basic accounting
  - Financial statement analysis
  - Business metrics
  - Financial decision-making

**Skillshare: "Business Finance Fundamentals"**
- **Cost:** $32/year (first month free)
- **Duration:** Self-paced, 2-3 weeks to core material
- **URL:** https://www.skillshare.com/
- **What's covered:**
  - P&L, balance sheet, cash flow
  - Key metrics
  - Financial planning

### Books

**"The Lean Enterprise" by James P. Womack**
- **Cost:** $20 (Amazon)
- **Best for:** Understanding how metrics and systems connect
- **Bonus:** Pairs well with Lean Six Sigma learning

**"Profit First" by Mike Michalowicz**
- **Cost:** $15 (Amazon)
- **Best for:** Owner/operator perspective (relevant for your consulting goal)
- **What it teaches:** Managing business finances, profit prioritization

### Spreadsheet Exercise (FREE)

**Build a Simple Restaurant P&L**
- Use Google Sheets or Excel
- Get a real P&L from a restaurant (ask your partner, or search online for "restaurant P&L template")
- Recreate it and add 10% to labor costs; calculate impact on profit
- This is real learning by doing

### Your Financial Literacy Timeline

- **Months 1-3:** Start with Khan Academy (free) + ask your partner (free)
- **Months 3-6:** Supplement with Udemy course ($13-50) or Skillshare ($0 with free trial)
- **Months 6-12:** Apply knowledge to your new role (measure impact of your work)
- **Total time investment:** 30-50 hours over 6 months

## Skill Gap 3: Process Mapping & Data Analysis

### Included in Lean Six Sigma Course

Process mapping and basic data analysis are covered in your Green Belt course:
- Value Stream Mapping (VSM)
- Process documentation (BPMN)
- Data collection and analysis
- Dashboard creation

**So you don't need separate courses.** Use these tools to supplement:

### Tools Practice (FREE/Low Cost)

**Process Mapping Tools**

**Lucidchart (Freemium)**
- **Cost:** Free (limited diagrams) or $12/month
- **URL:** https://www.lucidchart.com/
- **What for:** Creating process flow diagrams, org charts, swimlanes
- **Learn by doing:** Create a process map of your current role

**Draw.io (FREE)**
- **Cost:** FREE
- **URL:** https://www.draw.io/
- **What for:** Simple process diagrams (lighter than Lucidchart)
- **Bonus:** Works offline

**Miro (Freemium)**
- **Cost:** Free (limited boards) or $15/month
- **URL:** https://miro.com/
- **What for:** Digital whiteboarding, process mapping, team collaboration
- **Best for:** Working with teams on process design

### Data Analysis Tools

**Google Sheets (FREE)**
- **URL:** https://sheets.google.com
- **What to learn:**
  - Pivot tables
  - Charting (pie charts, line graphs, bar charts)
  - Basic formulas (SUM, AVERAGE, IF)
  - Conditional formatting
- **Learn:** YouTube tutorials for each skill (search "Google Sheets pivot table tutorial")
- **Time:** 8-10 hours total to learn core skills

**Excel (PAID, but likely already have)**
- Same as Google Sheets; most corporate jobs prefer Excel
- **YouTube:** "Excel Data Analysis Beginner to Advanced" (free tutorials)

**Tableau (Freemium)**
- **Cost:** Free (limited) or $15-70/month
- **URL:** https://www.tableau.com/
- **What for:** Advanced dashboards and data visualization
- **When to learn:** Year 2+ (after mastering Google Sheets)

### YouTube Learning (FREE)

**Google Sheets Tutorials (Free)**
- Search: "Google Sheets Pivot Table Tutorial"
- Search: "Google Sheets Charts and Graphs"
- Search: "Google Sheets Data Analysis for Beginners"
- Time: 2-3 hours total

**Process Mapping Tutorials (Free)**
- Search: "Value Stream Mapping Tutorial"
- Search: "Business Process Mapping Basics"
- Search: "How to Create a Process Flowchart"
- Time: 1-2 hours total

### Your Process Mapping Timeline

- **Months 1-3:** Free YouTube tutorials on Google Sheets (pivot tables, charting)
- **Months 3-6:** Practice creating dashboards in your new role
- **Months 6-9:** Lean Six Sigma Green Belt includes advanced process mapping
- **Months 9-12:** Apply DMAIC methodology to real projects
- **Total time investment:** 20-30 hours spread over 12 months

## Skill Gap 4: Consulting Portfolio

### Tools & Templates (Mostly Free)

**Portfolio Documentation**
- Use Google Docs (FREE) to write case studies
- Save in your Career/_case-studies/ folder
- Share with partner for feedback

**Consulting Pitch Deck**
- Use Google Slides (FREE) or Canva (Freemium)
- Template: 1-pager with case studies + your approach
- Export as PDF for sharing

**Simple Portfolio Website** (Optional, for Year 2)
- Webflow (Freemium with free tier)
- Wix (Freemium)
- Carrd (paid, but simple: $12/year)
- Or just use Google Doc + PDF

### Books on Consulting

**"The Business Model Handbook" by Alexander Osterwalder**
- **Cost:** $20 (Amazon)
- **Best for:** Understanding consulting business models
- **What it covers:** How to structure consulting services, pricing, delivery

**"Getting to Yes" by Fisher & Ury**
- **Cost:** $15 (Amazon)
- **Best for:** Negotiation and client conversations
- **What it covers:** Negotiation principles that help in consulting

**"The Consulting Bible" by Alan Weiss**
- **Cost:** $25 (Amazon)
- **Best for:** How to position and sell consulting services
- **What it covers:** Consulting business, pricing, client acquisition

### Courses on Consulting (Optional)

**Skillshare: "Become a Consultant in Your Field"**
- **Cost:** $32/year (first month free)
- **Duration:** 2-3 weeks
- **What it covers:** How to start, position, price, and land consulting clients

**Udemy: "Consulting Fundamentals"**
- **Cost:** $13-50
- **Duration:** 2-3 weeks
- **What it covers:** Consulting frameworks, client engagement, problem-solving

### Your Consulting Portfolio Timeline

- **Months 1-12:** Document case studies in your new role (no special resources needed)
- **Month 12:** Compile into portfolio (Google Docs + PDF)
- **Months 12-18:** Create simple 1-pager pitch (Google Slides)
- **Optional (Year 2+):** Simple website if you want (Carrd or similar)
- **Total cost:** $0-50 (if you do paid course; not necessary)
- **Total time:** 20-30 hours spread over 18 months

## Pro Tips

1. **Take advantage of free trials:** Skillshare, Udemy, Coursera offer free trials. Test before committing.
2. **Ask for discounts:** Lean Six Sigma Institute often has coupon codes. Google "Lean Six Sigma Institute coupon 2026"
3. **Your partner is your best resource:** Don't underestimate free mentorship from someone who knows these domains.
4. **Combine learning methods:** Video lectures + books + practice = deeper learning
5. **Apply immediately:** Take a concept on Monday, use it in your job on Wednesday. Real learning happens through application.
