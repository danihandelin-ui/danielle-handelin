## Path 1: Event Coordinator at Premium Venue

**Title:** Event Coordinator, Event Manager, Venue Coordinator, or Catering Sales Coordinator

**What you'd do:**

- Coordinate vendors (catering, rentals, AV, florals, security)
- Create timelines, budgets, floor plans
- Manage setup/breakdown logistics
- Lead on-site event execution
- Manage client relationships before/during/after event

**Compensation:**
- Hourly: $31-39/hour
- Salary equivalent: $64,480-$81,120/year
- Benefits: Typically yes (health insurance, time off)
- Travel: Some (depends on venue; most events at one location)

### Why You're Perfect For This

**Your exact experience:**
- ✅ Managed 30-500+ person events at Carboy Winery
- ✅ Handled vendor coordination, logistics, on-site execution
- ✅ Ensured "seamless internal operations and positive brand representation"
- ✅ "Front of House" background means you understand hospitality, service standards, guest experience
- ✅ Glendale Hospitality shows you can manage multiple stakeholders (servers, executives, customers)

**What to emphasize in your application:**
1. **Scale:** "Managed events from 30-500 people"
2. **Complexity:** "Coordinated [X] external vendors, managed logistics and timeline, led on-site execution"
3. **Quality:** "Delivered zero service failures; 100% positive client feedback"
4. **Independence:** "Managed all aspects of events with minimal supervision"

### Job Search Strategy for Event Coordinator

**Where to find jobs:**
- LinkedIn (search: "Event Coordinator" + your city + filter by "Country Club" or "Hotel")
- Indeed.com (search: "Event Coordinator [Your City]")
- Glassdoor (search: "Event Coordinator" + salary transparency shows actual ranges)
- CareerBuilder
- Hospitality-specific job boards: Hcareers.com, Poached Jobs
- Local country club websites (many post jobs locally first)

**How to apply:**

1. **Cover letter approach:**
   - "I'm an operations leader with 4+ years event management experience in hospitality settings."
   - "I've managed events from 30-500 people, coordinating vendors, timelines, and on-site execution."
   - "I bring strong operational thinking + hospitality background + attention to guest experience."
   - "I'm excited to transition into a coordinator role at [specific venue] to build my event management portfolio."

2. **Resume emphasis:**
   - Headline: "Event Coordinator | Operations Leader | Hospitality Background"
   - Bullet 1: "Managed 30-500+ person events, coordinating external vendors, creating timelines, and leading on-site execution"
   - Bullet 2: "Designed operational systems that scaled across locations and 200+ team members"
   - Bullet 3: "[Your latest: Jira architecture / Marketing / Contractor development], demonstrating systems thinking and ability to learn new platforms"

3. **Red flags to address:**
   - "Why are you leaving a Scrum Master role for an event coordinator role?"
     - **Answer:** "I'm transitioning from tech/agile into hospitality operations. I want to be in a role managing concrete problems (events, logistics, vendors) rather than abstract process management. This coordinator role positions me to build expertise in operations management at premium venues, which is my next career focus."
   - "Why are you jumping around (tech → hospitality → tech)?"
     - **Answer:** "I've had diverse experience, which has taught me how to adapt across industries. What ties it together is operations + systems thinking. I'm now focusing specifically on hospitality operations, and this role is the right fit."
