## FAQ

**Q: I feel like I'm not qualified for these ops roles. What if they say no?**
A: You are absolutely qualified. Read 01-Reposition.md to see why. The gap is positioning, not skills. Say "yes" to interview requests. Worst case: free practice interviews that make you stronger.

**Q: Should I learn Lean Six Sigma now or wait?**
A: Wait until you land your coordinator/ops role (Months 3-6). Use the free time before then for job search and financial literacy. Then do Green Belt certification (Month 6-9) while in your new role.

**Q: What if I get a job offer for less than $30/hr?**
A: Negotiate. You have 8+ years experience + Scrum cert. $30-40/hr is realistic for coordinator roles at premium venues. If they're offering less, keep looking.

**Q: Can I do this while keeping my current job?**
A: Yes. Months 0-6 (job search) can happen in your spare time. Once you land the coordinator role, the work becomes your day job. Then learning/consulting happens nights/weekends.

**Q: When should I start consulting?**
A: Month 12 minimum (after Green Belt + case studies). But you can start learning the consulting business earlier (read the Playbook in Month 6).

**Q: What's the first consulting project I should target?**
A: Something small ($3k-5k) at a venue/restaurant you know or a friend's business. Build case study and testimonial. Use that to pitch bigger projects.
