### Conversion + Loyalty Stages

**TLDR:** Get leads to buy by removing friction and proving value. Keep them coming back through intentional post-purchase engagement. Retention is cheaper than acquisition — optimize both.

**Key concepts:**
- **Conversion (decision/purchase stage):** Not everyone who considers will convert. Win them with clear product info, smooth checkout, obvious returns policy, well-placed ads, and an organized site. Make it easy to find answers and take action.
- **Loyalty stage:** Quality product gets them in the door; post-purchase experience brings them back. Use follow-up emails, rewards programs, social engagement. Personalize — a coupon for something they just bought is tone-deaf.
- **Both matter:** Acquisition without retention is expensive. The best businesses balance both.

**Applied to Trace Strategies:**
- [ ] Audit Trace's client-facing materials — do they clearly demonstrate value and make the next step obvious? (What's their "smooth checkout"?)
- [ ] Map the post-project client touchpoints — is there a follow-up process after an engagement ends? (Check-in email, case study share, retainer pitch?)
- [ ] Identify what "loyalty" looks like for a B2B consulting firm — repeat engagements, referrals, retainer contracts

**Applied to your portfolio/consulting:**
