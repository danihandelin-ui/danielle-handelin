## The 3 Consulting Models (Then Your Hybrid)

**Project-Based** — hired for a specific engagement, $10–50k per project, 3 weeks to 3 months. High fees, high variability. Great variety, but constant sales required.

**Retainer (Fractional Ops Director)** — $3–10k/month, ongoing relationship, 10–20 hrs/week per client. Predictable income, lower sales pressure. Can serve 2–4 clients at once.

**Product/IP (Course, Playbook, Framework)** — create once, sell indefinitely. Slow to start, passive once built. No travel, scalable beyond your hours. High upfront effort.

**Your recommended path (in this order):**
1. **Year 1:** Land a retainer or project engagement at a premium venue. Build deep case studies.
2. **Year 1–2:** Create one product (playbook or course) based on your most common engagement type.
3. **Year 2+:** 1–2 retainer clients for stability + occasional projects + product revenue.

*Why this order: You need stable income now. Deep client relationships will teach you more than theory. Your product will be more authentic and useful if it's built from real work.*

### Gap 2: Financial Literacy — Months 1–3

**The problem:** You understand operations but not financial impact. Consulting clients ask "what's the ROI?"

*Example: "I redesigned FOH onboarding, reducing ramp time by 2 days."*
*Client: "So what's the ROI?"*
*Better answer: "Reducing ramp time by 2 days saves ~$X per new hire in training labor. Across 200+ hires annually, that's $X in savings — roughly a 10x return on the system design investment."*

**What to learn:**
- P&L structure: revenue, COGS, labor cost, overhead, net profit
- Labor cost %: what it means, why it matters, how to move it
- ROI calculation: investment ÷ annual savings = payback period
- How to build a simple restaurant P&L in Google Sheets

**How to learn (all free):**
- Ask your partner: "Teach me to read a P&L and calculate labor economics" — 2–4 hours
- Khan Academy Finance: khanacademy.org/finance-and-capital-markets — 10–15 hours
- Practice project: Build a simple restaurant P&L from sample data in Sheets

**Cost:** $0 | **Time:** 10–20 hours over 4–6 weeks

---

### Gap 3: Excel + Process Mapping — Months 1–2

**Excel** — 83% of ops roles require it; you use Sheets but it's not the same on resumes.

What to learn: SUM, AVERAGE, IF, VLOOKUP, pivot tables, basic charts, simple dashboards.

How to learn:
- YouTube: Leila Gharani or Chandoo channels (free, 12–16 hours)
- Or Udemy "Excel for Business Analysis" (~$15 on sale, more structured)

Practice projects:
- [ ] Build a restaurant P&L from sample data
- [ ] Create a pivot table from a dataset (staff hours, costs, event data)
- [ ] Build a 3–4 KPI dashboard (labor cost %, revenue per FTE, turnover)

**Process Mapping** — clients need to *see* the problem. You can diagnose; now make it visual.

Tools: Lucidchart (free tier), draw.io (free), Miro

What to learn: Map current state → identify bottlenecks → design future state. This is what separates a coordinator from a consultant.

How to learn: 2–3 hours of YouTube tutorials, then apply it to a real project at your new role.

**Cost:** $0–15 | **Time:** 15–20 hours total

### Gap 4: Consulting Portfolio — Months 1–12, ongoing

Without documented case studies, you're just talking. With them, you're a consultant.

**Target: 3–4 polished case studies by Month 12.**

**Case Study Template (use this within 1 week of completing any significant project):**

```
PROJECT: [Name]
CLIENT: [Venue/Organization]
DATE: [MM/YYYY]

SITUATION:
[1–2 sentences: What was broken and why it was a business problem]

DIAGNOSIS:
[1–2 sentences: How you identified the root cause]

SOLUTION:
[1–2 sentences: What you designed and implemented]

RESULTS:
- [Metric: e.g., "Onboarding time reduced by 28%"]
- [Metric: e.g., "Event execution failures dropped from 35% to 5%"]
- [Financial impact estimate if possible]

METHODOLOGY:
[Brief: process analysis, stakeholder interviews, iterative design, change management]

TAKEAWAY:
[1 sentence: The transferable insight]
```

**How to build the portfolio while working:**
1. Within 1 week of finishing a project → fill in the template (30 min while details are fresh)
2. At 3 months → add follow-up metrics, polish language to "consulting speak"
3. By Month 9 → 3–4 drafts complete
4. By Month 12 → polished and ready for pitch

**Starting case studies from your existing experience:**
- FOH Onboarding System (Carboy, 4 locations, 200+ staff, 28% faster ramp)
- Operational Reporting Dashboard (Glendale, 3 restaurants, real-time exec visibility)
- Jira Governance System (Trace, automated dashboards + gated launch rules)
- CRM Implementation (BBBS, org-wide donor management replacing manual process)
