**Target venues:** Country clubs, luxury hotels (Four Seasons, Ritz, Marriott, Hilton), dedicated event spaces, upscale fine dining with private dining programs.

**What to emphasize in applications:**
1. Scale: "Managed events from 30–500 people."
2. Complexity: "Coordinated [X] external vendors, managed logistics and timeline, led on-site execution."
3. Independence: "Managed all event aspects with minimal supervision."
4. Systems thinking: "I build processes so events run without heroics."

**Cover letter angle:**
> "I'm an operations leader with 4+ years event management experience in hospitality. I've managed events from 30–500 people, coordinating vendors, timelines, and day-of execution. I bring strong operational thinking, hospitality background, and attention to guest experience. I'm excited to transition into a coordinator role at [venue] to build my event management portfolio."

**Red flag to address:** "Why are you leaving a Scrum Master role for event coordination?"
> "I'm returning to hospitality operations because I want concrete, tangible problems — events, logistics, vendors — not abstract process management. This role positions me for what I want long-term: ops leadership at premium venues."

**Search terms:** Event Coordinator, Venue Coordinator, Wedding Coordinator, Catering Manager, Private Dining Manager

**Job boards:** LinkedIn, Indeed, Hcareers.com, Poached Jobs, local country club websites

**Target venues:** Country clubs (Ops Manager, AGM), luxury hotels (F&B Ops Manager, Guest Services Manager), upscale event venues (Venue Manager, Director of Operations).

**What to emphasize:**
1. Systems design: "Designed FOH onboarding standards adopted organization-wide across 4 locations and 200+ staff."

3. Metrics: "Created operational reporting systems providing real-time executive visibility."
4. Implementation: "Led CRM, Jira, and contractor onboarding rollouts — not just design, but execution."

**Interview answer — "How would you approach optimizing our FOH operations?"**
> "I'd start by observing current processes and talking to the people doing the work — not just managers. I've done this across 4 locations and 200+ staff, so I know how to make changes stick."

**Search terms:** Operations Manager, Operations Coordinator, Assistant General Manager, Venue Operations Manager + hospitality, country club, hotel
