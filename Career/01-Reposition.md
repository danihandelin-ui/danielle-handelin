## Exercise 2: Red Flag Detection—Where Are You Underselling?

Read the reformatted statements above. Which ones feel like you're *underplaying* what you actually contributed?

### Common underselling patterns (check any that apply):

- [ ] **Vague ownership:** "Helped with" vs "Led" or "Designed"
- [ ] **Inherited credit:** "Used the system our director created" vs "Created the system that..."
- [ ] **Downplaying scope:** Mentioning 1 location when you managed 4; mentioning "events" when you managed $XXX in logistics
- [ ] **Passive voice:** "Reports were created" vs "Created reports that..."
- [ ] **Minimizing impact:** "Improved things" vs "Reduced onboarding time by 2 days, saving $XXXX"
- [ ] **Framing as "just" your job:** "I just coordinated events" vs "I managed complex multi-vendor logistics at scale"

### Danielle's specific patterns (from your resume):

1. **Operations work framed as "coordination"**
   - You didn't just coordinate events. You designed event systems.
   - You didn't just manage FOH. You architected onboarding standards that scaled across 4 locations.

2. **Systems work understated**
   - Installing a donor CRM is a systems implementation project, not just "organizing donors"
   - Building Jira architecture is not "using Jira"—it's designing governance systems

3. **Growth/impact numbers missing**
   - "4x Instagram engagement" is quantified ✅
   - "2 days faster onboarding" is quantified ✅
   - Missing: revenue impact, labor savings, error reduction
