Role Pathway: Your 18-Month Plan

Phase 1: Now (0-3 months)

Complete resume and LinkedIn
Target 10-15 venues for event coordinator or ops coordinator roles
Start learning financial literacy (free resources)

Phase 2: Land Your Role (3-6 months)

Land $30-40/hr coordinator or operations role
Succeed in first 90 days; understand operations deeply
Start documenting projects as case studies

Phase 3: Skill Up (6-9 months)

Enroll in and complete Lean Six Sigma Green Belt
Complete 3-4 case studies from your ops role
Create consulting pitch/1-pager

Phase 4: Launch Consulting (9-18 months)

Land first 1-2 consulting clients ($3k-15k projects)
Reach $50/hr income (consulting + ops role or higher ops role)
[Optional] Create first product (course, playbook)
