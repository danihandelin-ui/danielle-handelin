Reference — Stretches, Workouts & Yoga

Pre-Run Dynamic Warm-Up (~5 min)

  • Leg swings front/back — 10 each leg
  • Leg swings side to side — 10 each leg
  • Hip circles — 10 each direction
  • High knees — 20 steps
  • Butt kicks — 20 steps
  • Ankle circles — 10 each
  • Walking lunges — 10 steps
  • Arm circles — 10 forward, 10 back

Post-Run Static Cool-Down (~8–10 min)

  • Standing quad stretch — 30 sec each
  • Standing calf stretch — 30 sec each
  • Seated hamstring stretch — 45 sec each
  • Pigeon pose — 60 sec each side
  • Hip flexor lunge stretch — 30 sec each
  • Figure-4 glute stretch — 30 sec each
  • Child's pose — 60 sec
  • Seated spinal twist — 30 sec each

Strength Workout A — Lower Body Focus

  • Goblet squats — 3×12
  • Romanian deadlifts (dumbbells) — 3×10
  • Reverse lunges — 3×10 each leg
  • Glute bridges — 3×15
  • Single-leg deadlift (bodyweight) — 3×8 each
  • Calf raises — 3×20
  • Dead bug — 3×10 each side
  • Plank — 3×30–45 sec

Strength Workout B — Full Body / Core

  • Dumbbell sumo squats — 3×12
  • Dumbbell rows — 3×10 each
  • Step-ups (use a chair/step) — 3×10 each
  • Push-ups — 3×10 (modify as needed)
  • Hip thrusts (bodyweight) — 3×15
  • Side-lying leg raises — 3×15 each
  • Russian twists — 3×12 each
  • Superman hold — 3×10

Yoga with Adriene — Phase 1 Picks

  • "Yoga for Runners" — post-run recovery
  • "Yoga for Hips" — great after long runs
  • "Yoga for Lower Back Relief" — if back is tight
  • "Morning Yoga — 20 Min" — any active rest day
  • "Yoga for Athletes" — general maintenance
  • "Yin Yoga" — deep holds, ideal Friday nights
  • "Yoga for Beginners" — lighter weeks/cycle week
  • "Yoga for Stress Relief" — rough days

Strides (add to end of easy runs)

  • After your run, walk 1 min to catch breath
  • Run 20 sec at 80% effort — not a sprint
  • Walk 40–60 sec full recovery
  • Repeat 4–6 times total
  • Should feel fast but controlled, not gasping
  • Add strides to Tuesday runs from Week 2 on
